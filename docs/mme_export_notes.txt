-= Messages (added NMR 1.8.4) =-
Full client handoff (how each row is built and how to match and use the rows): docs/MME_MESSAGES_HANDOFF.md.
MegaMud: see docs/MEGAMUD_MME_MESSAGES_HANDOFF.md for mapping these rows onto its message records and flags.
One row per game line a client may need to recognise. Not a dump of the realm's message file:
message numbers, caster-only lines of monster spells, room lines and room sounds are not exported, and lines that
follow the standard formats listed further down are left out because clients already parse
those, except the cast line of a spell players or items cast, which is always exported.

Columns: ID (row id), Name, Kind, Msg, EndMsg, Effects, Spell (Long: the number of the spell the row
belongs to, 0 when none; see Name). Bracket column names in SQL ([Name],
[Msg]): Name is a Jet reserved word, as in the other MME tables. Empty string columns are written
as a single Chr(0) character (same as the other MME tables).

Kind (who the line is about, from the player's seat):
  Cast   confirmation that MY spell or item worked, with the amount when there is one: "You fire a
         magic missile at {target} for {dmg} damage!", "A cool wind blows throughout the room!",
         "You cast agony on {target} for {dmg} damage!". Every spell players or items cast has its
         cast line here, standard form or not. When that line is a standard form (or empty), the
         start line is exported as well ("You lay hands on {target}."). Both are printed only once
         the spell has landed. Name and Spell = the spell, also when an item casts it.
  Self   something happened TO ME. With an EndMsg it is a tracked effect: Msg is printed when the
         effect starts (and by STAT), EndMsg when it wears off. Without an EndMsg it is a one-off
         line: incoming damage in a non-standard wording, a failed action (Effects contains Fail),
         a monster hit with a non-standard verb phrase (Effects contains Monster), a trap
         (Effects contains Trap), my damage shield or my item's spikes hitting back
         (DamageShield), or a spell a textblock, cast exit or spell trap casts on me ("You suffer
         in the desert heat... you need water, soon!"; Effects = the spell's, plus Trap for spell
         traps), or an incoming spell line whose status message lacks a status or a wear-off text
         (when it has both, the status row already shows the effect starting and ending). Name =
         the spell (and Spell its number) for effect and shield rows of friendly player or item
         spells, the item for item damage-shield rows; the text for lines a client matches by text
         alone.
  Other  something happened to someone else that a client may track: a monster hitting another
         player in a non-standard wording (Damage,Monster), or a monster's death text (Death,
         Name = the monster), or a fixed line from the stock
         game: someone leaving through a command exit, "{target} slips into the dark alley."
         (Chase, so a client can follow a party member), and a monster appearing or changing form,
         "A moss zombie rises from the ground!" (CheckRoom: look at the room again). The Chase and
         CheckRoom rows are the same in every export.

Name: Cast rows carry the spell's name, and Spell its number, also when an item casts the spell: items
get no rows of their own. An item's lines are the rows whose Spell is in its UseSpells / HitSpells (see
Items below), plus the rows of the spells those chain to through EndCast (Spells ability 151, followed
transitively). Self rows carry the spell's name and number only when the player can be the one affected
by their own spell or item (self, friendly or party target types). Every spell players or items cast gets
its own rows under its own number, even when several spells print the same line ("You feel lucky!" is
exported once as bless and once as chant; items that cast bless list it in UseSpells). Join on Spell,
not on Name: spells can share a name. Item damage-shield rows (Spell 0) carry the item's name. A spell
counts as
player-castable when the export's cross-referencing marks it Learnable; an "Export Only" run
without that data falls back to "has a magery type". Every
other row (status and damage lines of enemy-targeted or monster-only spells, monster hits, traps,
death texts shared by differently named monsters) is realm-wide and is named after its own text, shortened: a leading
"The {source} " or "{source} ", a trailing " for {dmg} damage!", " {dmg} damage!" or " {dmg} hit
points!" and any "{target} " or "{target}'s " are dropped (so "The {source} claws you with its
pincers for {dmg} damage!" is named "claws you with its pincers"; "{source}'s " and "The {source}'s "
are dropped the same way). Name is never empty. Rows are unique by kind, Spell (Name for rows without a
spell), Msg and EndMsg: a line printed by several sources of the same spell or name (or by several
nameless sources) is exported once with the union of their Effects. A Self line that is the "you" form of a Cast or Other line
("{target}" read as "you", "{target}'s" as "your") is not exported separately: clients must accept
"you"/"your" wherever {target} appears and treat the line as being about themselves when it does.
Death texts are exported only for monsters that regenerate or are game-limited and award more
than 100 experience (EXP times ExpMulti in the Monsters table). Colour codes embedded in a message are stripped.

Tokens: {source} (the caster or attacking monster, as the game prints it, including any "The"),
{target}, {dmg}, {attacker} (the one who hit a damage shield). Match them as wildcards; {dmg} is
an integer. The spell name is written literally. For area, room and party spells the engine
prints nothing where {target} would be, and the exported text already reflects that ("You cast
greater healing rain on your party, healing {dmg} damage!"). "{?}" marks a placeholder NMR could
not identify (the realm's data is inconsistent there).

Placeholder order, should a client want to rebuild the engine's raw line: Cast = spell name,
{target}, {dmg} (a start line: {target} for every placeholder); Self from a textblock, cast exit
or spell trap = spell name, {target}, {dmg}; damage shield = {attacker}, {dmg}; Self from a cast = {source}, spell name, {dmg}; Self/EndMsg from a status
message = none or spell name; monster hit on me = {source}, {dmg}; monster hit on another =
{source}, {target}, {dmg} (the engine prints that damage through a string placeholder, so accept
digits wherever {dmg} appears); trap = {dmg} if present. Spells whose message style byte selects
the short form use spell-less orders ({target}, {dmg} / {dmg}); exports made with "For GreaterMUD
Engine" checked follow that engine's reading of the style byte (exact value 1), stock exports use
bit 0.

Effects (CSV): Poisoned Confused Blinded Held Fear Mute Sleep Damage DamageOverTime Drain Heal
  HealOverTime HPRegen ManaRegen ManaHeal ManaSteal DamageShield Cure (cure poison, dispel,
  freedom, remove curse) Fail RoomSpell Monster Trap Death Chase CheckRoom. Disease = DamageOverTime plus stat drains; "cannot attack" = Sleep or Held; a timed
  effect is any Self row with an EndMsg.

The export writes NMR-Log_MMEMessages.txt next to the MDB only when something needed attention:
a placeholder that could not be mapped ({?}), a missing message record, a blank spell name, or a
value truncated to its column size.

-= Standard formats clients must implement themselves =-
These are the engine's fixed wordings and the shapes that every custom message follows. A client
needs them hard-coded; the Messages table only carries what deviates from them, plus the cast line
of every spell players or items cast (a Cast row under the spell's name and number, whatever its form).
<X> = any text, <N> = a number, <verb> = a single word.

Damage to me / to others (line colour: bold red, bold blue or cyan for monster attacks):
  <attacker> <verb> you for <N> damage!            <attacker> <verb> <target> for <N> damage!
  <attacker> [critically|savagely|viciously|skillfully|quickly|all-out|surprise] <verb> ...
  You are <X> for <N> damage!                      You take <N> damage <X>
  <X> you for <N> damage        (any incoming line with this phrase is a damage attack on me)
  <X> <target> for <N> damage   (any line with this phrase is a damage attack on someone else)
  <attacker> casts <X> on|at|upon you for <N> damage!
  <target> <X> <N> <X> damage!   (any third-party observation of damage or healing)
Misses and dodges:
  <attacker> <verb> at <target> ... [but you dodge|sidestep|easily dodge|barely dodge|
  quickly roll|leap out of|duck the attack]        You quickly backpedal ...
  ... at you / but misses / and barely misses you! / scrambles to regain balance /
  looking for an opening! / an opportunity to strike!      <X> ... but ... / misses / glances
My attacks:
  You <verb> <target> for <N> damage!              Your inaccurate <X> for <N> damage!
  <X> ... your foe(s)/enemy/enemies/opponent(s)/adversary/adversaries ... <N> damage
  You deliver a <X> to <target>                    Your <X> glances off <target>!
  You miss / swing at / lunge at / fire at / shoot at / double-shoot at / hurl at / hurl your /
  flail at / lash at / snap at / spray at / swipe at / thrust at <target>
  ... and barely miss ... / You stumble and / You slip and scramble / You circle and /
  You advance, looking ...                         You punch / You kick / You jumpkick (no damage)
  You [feint and|spin and|leap and|lunge and|roll and|duck and|skillfully|quickly] surprise
  <target>   ... critically ...                    You cannot backstab with <X>
  Your weapon has no effect ...   Your fists have no effect ...   Your command had no effect
  You need a weapon <X>
Engagement:
  <X> moves to attack you!   <X> moves to attack <Y>   <X> moves to attack everyone in the room
  <X> moves to cast <spell> on|at|upon <Y>   <X> breaks off combat   <X> moves to protect <Y>
  <X> tries to rob you
Death, kills, experience:
  You have been killed!   But, due to a miracle, you have been saved   <X> drops to the ground!
  <X> is dead.   You gain <N> experience
  You have <N> lives left.   You feel your life-force ebbing.
Confusion:
  You look around stupidly and do nothing!   (a confused player's command fails)
Spell casting:
  You cast|invoke|sing <spell> [on <target>] ...   You attempt to cast|invoke|sing <spell> ...
  on yourself / on you / on <your name> / on <party member>
  You may not cast that   Why would you want to cast <X>   Your spell has no effect
  There are no more uses <X>   You have already cast|invoked <X>
  You cast <spell> on <target>, healing <N> damage!   (HP comes from the prompt, not from N)
  You cast <spell> on <target> for <N> damage!       (and the on|at|upon variants)
Status and state:
  You feel ill   You feel sick   You are too sick to rest   (poison)
  The effects of <X> / You are no longer <X> / Your are no longer <X> / <X> returns to <X> /
  <X> wear off / <X> wears off          (wear-off keywords; the exact EndMsg is in the table)
  You are no longer sneaking   You are now resting   You are now meditating
  You awake from deep meditation feeling stronger
  The room is pitch black / very dark / dimly lit / barely visible
  To do this action, <X>   You are overcome with a feeling of guilt

-= Items (added NMR 1.8.4) =-
HitVerbs / HitVerbsOther / MissVerbs (items with ItemType 1 = Weapon only, every weapon class including
1H Blunt; empty on every other item): "|"-separated verb alternatives used in
"You <HitVerbs> X for N damage!", "<player> <HitVerbsOther> X for N damage!" and misses.
UseSpells / HitSpells (every item type): the spells the item casts when used ("|"-separated spell numbers) and on hit
("|"-separated "<spell>:<chance %>" pairs), decoded from the Abil-n columns: a CastsSp (43) is cast on use unless a
%Spell (114) comes before it, which makes it the on-hit spell with that chance. Slot order, no repeats; empty when none.
UseCode: "#" + up to 4 lowercase letters, only on items with a use spell (none on items that only cast on hit). Made from
the name: one word = its first 4 letters; several words = their initials, the last initial replaced by the whole last
word when there are fewer than 4 ("jade amulet" = #jamu); possessive 's dropped; a, an, the, of skipped. Items with
the same name share a code; for a differently named item that wants a taken code NMR tries the first 2 or 3 letters
of the first word plus the other initials and the last word ("black potion" = #blpo next to "blue potion" = #bpot),
then any 4 letters of the name in order, digits last. Lower item numbers keep the natural code. Updating an export
keeps every code it already holds for its item name. A command hint, not a key: key items by Number. Clients that
treat item uses like spells: one entry per item with a UseCode (name = Name, stats from the UseSpells rows); its
confirming lines are the Messages Cast rows whose Spell is one of its UseSpells or reached from them through EndCast,
and its on-hit lines come from HitSpells the same way.

-= Titles (added NMR 1.8.4) =-
One row per class, so a client can tell a player's level range from their title. Columns: Class (the class
number, = Classes.Number) and Titles, the class's title textblock written as comma-separated level ranges
"<first>-<last>:<title>" in level order:
  1-1:Apprentice,2-4:Warrior Novice,5-9:Grunt,...,45-49:Lord|Lady,...,70-109:Dragonslayer
Line N of the textblock is the title at level N; neighbouring levels with the same title share one range.
"Male|Female" is kept as the game writes it: the part before "|" is the male title, the part after it the
female one, and a title without "|" is used for both. ANSI colour codes, other control characters and the
spaces around each title are removed (GreaterMUD colours its level 100+ titles).
The last range ends at the textblock's last line, which differs by class (99 to 118 lines in stock data).
The textblock has no title for higher levels; NMR does not know what the game prints there, so treat the
last range as open-ended.
Titles repeat across classes ("Apprentice" is level 1 for every stock class) and can repeat within a class
in a custom realm, so look a title up in the player's class when it is known and accept every range it
appears in. Titles is empty (a single Chr(0)) for a class without a title textblock.
Parsing: split on ","; an entry's range is the text before its first ":", the title is everything after it.
Stock and GreaterMUD titles contain no "," or ":". Should a custom realm's title contain a ",", a piece that
does not start with "<digits>-<digits>:" belongs to the entry before it.

-= Abilities (added NMR 1.8.4) =-
One row per ability in NMR's loaded ability list (ability.mdb, as edited in NMR's ability editor), so a client
can name the Abil-# columns of the other tables. Columns: Number (the ability number, = the Abil-# values;
primary key) and Name. Every row of the list is exported, including 0 ("empty"). The table is rewritten on
every export (an "Export Only ..." run only with "Ability List" checked); it is left as it was if NMR could
not open ability.mdb.
